🏠 Hero & Landing

🍞 Browse the Menu

Scroll down to see the sticky category ribbon. Click on any category — Bread, Cake, Pastry, Cookies, or Beverages — to filter the menu. Each card features real Unsplash photos, descriptions, allergen tags, and a glowing "+" button to add items to your cart.

Category Filtering Real Photos Glassmorphism Cards Hover Animations Allergen Tags

🎂 Custom Cake Builder

Design your dream cake! Choose from multiple tiers, each with shape, size, flavor, filling, and decoration options. Watch the visual cake preview update in real-time with stacking animation. Add a custom message and see live price calculation.

Multi-Tier Builder Live Preview 7 Flavors Real-Time Pricing Demo Video

🛒 Smart Cart

Click the cart icon in the navbar to open the sliding cart sidebar. Manage quantities, remove items, and see real-time totals. Your cart persists across sessions using localStorage.

Sliding Sidebar Quantity Controls Local Storage Auto Totals

💳 UPI Payment

Click "Proceed to Checkout" to open the premium UPI payment modal. Choose from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM UPI with proper SVG brand icons. View the QR code with gradient border, copy the UPI ID, or enter your own UPI ID to pay.

Google Pay PhonePe Paytm BHIM UPI QR Code Animated Progress

📷 QR Scanner

In the payment modal, click "Open Camera Scanner" to launch the full-screen QR scanner. It opens your device camera with animated scan line, corner brackets, and status indicator. Use Flash, Switch camera, or Demo mode to simulate a successful scan.
